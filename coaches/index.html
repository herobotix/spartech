Coaches Corner

Everything behind how our meetings run — the task cards students pick from, the plan they ladder up to, and the printables that make it work in the room. Built for Team 22257 coaches and mentors, and open to anyone on the team who wants to see what is coming.

How a meeting works

Every meeting has the same three parts. What changes is the length of the middle.

10 min
Orientation
Together. What happened last time, then each crew claims a card out loud.
100 / 160
Task time
Every card is built to fill 100 minutes — a whole Tuesday. Friday runs 12–3, so it gives you your card plus another hour.
10 min
Cleanup & report
Shop back to the reference photo. Each crew gives a 30-second report on what got checked off.
