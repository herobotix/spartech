The Plan

Four meetings, then two weeks of fall break. Everything below works backward from one goal: a drivable robot base attempting the Fast Lane Challenge on Friday, September 25.

The first four meetings

The student robot is the REV Mecanum Drivetrain Kit v2 — drivetrain only, and no intake is needed for Fast Lane. Steps marked ▶ critical cannot slip.

135–160: Run Fast Lane. Everyone drives at least one lap.

If you fall behind. The two checkpoints that decide everything are BA-2 finished Sept 18 and BA-3 finished Sept 22. If either slips, move people off the media and merch cards onto the build. Those cards keep; the deadline does not.

If the 25th arrives and it does not drive, run Fast Lane anyway — tape the track, walk the chassis around by hand, let drivers practice on the dev board. Forward and turning alone are enough for Fast Lane; if strafing is fighting you, ship the tank-style version and fix it after break.

Before you walk out on Sept 25. Two weeks is long enough to forget everything. Leave the robot in a known state, get every crew’s notebook entry written, and match the shop to the reference photo.

What can run at the same time

Every card is tagged with the hardware it needs. That tag is what decides how many crews can work at once.

TagCardsWhat it means for planning
No hardware35Runs anywhere, any number of crews at once. Planning, all eight CAD cards, merch, shirts, marketing, plus PR-9 (simulator) and BA-1/BA-6.
Programming board4Needs the 3D-printed dev board. PR-10, PR-11 and PR-12 teach touch, colour and potentiometer — none of which exist on the drivetrain kit.
Board or robot3Either works. Run these on the board while the build crew still has the robot apart.
Robot / Robot + field18The scarce resource — one crew at a time, unless the Herobotix class robot is present and has its own hub.

Control Hub accounting. The mecanum kit ships without a Control Hub, a battery or wiring. The team has two hubs: one is on the programming board, which leaves one for the student robot. That is exactly what the two-track plan needs — neither ever gets unbolted to run a meeting. No hub on the student robot means no Fast Lane.

Stickers & awards

When a card is stamped, everyone who worked on it marks one box in that category. Three boxes earns that category’s sticker. Awards are handed out at the last meeting before each break, not the moment they are earned. Stickers are printed by the team; the one exception is FULL STACK, an embroidered patch for the team shirt.
